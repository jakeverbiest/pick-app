## Description

PICK turns trash pickup into something you can see, measure, and share.

Drop your phone in your pocket, grab your picker, and go. PICK's motion detection counts every pickup automatically — no buttons, no tapping with gloves on, no breaking your rhythm. When you're done, your walk is on the map, your impact is tallied, and the sidewalks you cleaned turn green for everyone to see.

WHY PICKERS LOVE IT

• Hands-free counting — motion sensors detect each pickup while the phone stays in your pocket, screen off
• Sidewalk-level coverage — your neighborhood's actual sidewalks, colored by how recently they were cleaned. Green fades back to red over two weeks, so the map always shows where help is needed
• Real impact numbers — pickups become pounds become trash bags, calibrated by real scale weigh-ins, not guesses
• It counts as exercise — every cleanup logs to Apple Health as a walking workout, with distance and calories toward your rings
• Teams and challenges — school groups, volunteer days, and friendly neighborhood rivalries
• Privacy-minded — location is tracked only during active cleanups, sensor data never leaves your phone, and you can delete your account and data in-app anytime

THE NEIGHBORHOOD GAME

PICK's map is shared. When anyone cleans a block, it turns green for everyone — and slowly fades as days pass. The goal isn't to clean a street once; it's to keep a neighborhood green together.

Built by a picker, for pickers. Gloves on. 🛍️

## Review notes (for the App Review team)
PICK detects trash pickups via accelerometer/gyroscope while walking. To test: create an account, tap Start Cleanup, grant location + motion permissions, make a few bend-and-reach motions with the phone in hand (set Settings → Carry Mode → In hand), tap Stop, Save. Background location is used only during an active cleanup session to track the cleanup route with the screen off, per UIBackgroundModes location. Account deletion: Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account.
