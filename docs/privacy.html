PICK Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

PICK ("the App") is a community cleanup tracking app operated by Jake Verbiest ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account information. When you sign up we collect your display name, email address, neighborhood/zone name, and optional team or event name.

Cleanup session data. When you start a cleanup session, the App records: the number of pickups detected, session duration, estimated and self-reported trash weight, the date and time of the session, and GPS location data — including your walking route and the approximate locations of detected pickups. Route and session data are stored in our cloud database (Google Firebase) so your history, stats, maps, and leaderboards work across devices.

Motion sensor data. The App uses your phone's accelerometer and gyroscope to detect pickup motions. Raw sensor readings are processed on your device in real time and are not transmitted or stored — only the resulting detection events are kept.

Weight calibration data. If you weigh your collected trash and enter the measurement, it is stored on your device to improve weight estimates. This data stays on your device.

Apple Health. If you enable it, the App writes each cleanup to Apple Health as a walking workout (duration, distance, calories). The App does not read any data from Apple Health. You can disable this in Settings at any time.

What we do not collect. No contacts, photos, microphone audio, or advertising identifiers. We do not sell your data and do not show ads. Location is only tracked during an active cleanup session.

How we use data

To operate the App: counting pickups, drawing route maps and neighborhood coverage, calculating stats, streaks, badges, team totals, leaderboards, and challenges. Your display name and aggregate stats may appear on leaderboards visible to other users. Street-cleaning status (which sidewalk segments were cleaned, and when) is shared across all users to power the community coverage map.

Where data is stored

Account and session data are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), subject to Google's security practices. A local copy is cached on your device so the App works offline. Calibration samples and login state are stored only on your device.

Your choices

Delete your data: Settings → Danger Zone → "Clear All Data".
Delete your account: Settings → Danger Zone → "Delete Account" — removes your account and all associated data permanently, in the App, with no email required.
Location permission: deny or revoke in your phone settings; the App still counts pickups but cannot map routes.
Export: any session's data can be exported from the App.

Children

PICK is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy here and update the date above. Material changes will be announced in the App.
