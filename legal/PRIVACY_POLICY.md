PICK ("the App") is a community cleanup tracking app operated by Jake Verbiest ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## What We Collect

**Account information.** When you sign up we collect your display name, email address, neighborhood/zone name, and optional team or event name.

**Cleanup session data.** When you start a cleanup session, the App records: the number of pickups detected, session duration, estimated and self-reported trash weight, the date and time of the session, and GPS location data — including your walking route (a series of GPS points) and the approximate locations of detected pickups. Route and session data are stored in our cloud database (Google Firebase) so your history, stats, maps, and leaderboards work across devices.

**Motion sensor data.** The App uses your phone's accelerometer and gyroscope to detect pickup motions. Raw sensor readings (individual accelerometer/gyroscope samples) are processed on your device in real time and are never transmitted or stored anywhere — only a compact summary of each detected motion event (its strength, duration, and whether it was accepted as a pickup) is kept, alongside the route and session data described above.

**Weight calibration data.** If you weigh your collected trash and enter the measurement, the App stores the measurement on your device to improve weight estimates. This data stays on your device.

**What we do not collect.** We do not collect contacts, photos, microphone audio, advertising identifiers, or precise location while a cleanup session is not running. We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.

## How We Use Data

We use your data to operate the App: counting pickups, drawing your route maps and neighborhood heatmaps, calculating stats, streaks, badges, team totals, leaderboards, and challenges. Team leaderboards display aggregate team statistics; your display name may appear on leaderboards visible to other users.

If you enable a fitness app integration, session activity (duration, intensity) may be shared with the fitness service you choose.

## Where Data Is Stored

Account and session data are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), subject to Google's security practices. A local copy is cached on your device so the App works offline. Calibration samples and login state are stored only on your device.

## Your Choices

**Delete your data.** Settings → Danger Zone → "Clear All Data" permanently deletes your cleanups, stats, and badges. To delete your account entirely, email us at the address below.

**Location permission.** You can deny or revoke location access in your phone settings; the App will still count pickups but cannot map routes.

**Export.** You can export any session's data from the App.

## Children

PICK is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

We will post any changes to this policy here and update the date above. Material changes will be announced in the App.
